# Categories, favorites and tags

Three ways to organize the vault, which combine.

## Categories

Each entry belongs to a category: **Personal**, **Work**, **Finance**,
**Social** or **Other**. The sidebar filters by category with one click.
Use them to separate large blocks of your digital life.

## Favorites

Mark as a favorite (star icon) what you access all the time. The
**Favorites** shortcut in the sidebar shows only those.

## Tags

Tags are free-form labels you create (for example `client-x`, `shared`,
`change-password`). An entry can have several. The tag filter at the top
of the list crosses with the search and the category.

## Search and sort

- Search covers service, username, tags and notes.
- The list header sorts by service, username, category or strength.
- **Recent** lists what you opened last; **Trash** keeps deleted entries
  until you empty it.

## Bulk selection

Select several rows to favorite, tag or move to trash all at once.
