# Password generator

The generator creates strong random passwords. Open it from the key button
in the sidebar, or directly on an entry's form.

## Options

- **Length**: the longer, the stronger. For most services, 16 characters
  or more.
- **Character types**: uppercase, lowercase, digits and symbols. Turn
  symbols off only when the service doesn't accept them.
- The strength meter shows in real time how resistant the password is.

## Use the generated password

- On the **New password** or **Edit** screen, the generator fills the
  password field directly.
- Elsewhere, the generator has a copy button. The clipboard is cleared
  automatically after a few seconds.

## Good practice

- A **different password for each service**. That way, a breach at one
  site doesn't compromise the others.
- Don't try to memorize generated passwords — that's what the vault is
  for.
- The [Security report](seguranca) points out weak or reused passwords
  worth changing.
