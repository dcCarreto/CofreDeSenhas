# Appearance and accessibility

Everything here lives in **Settings**, split into tabs.

## Appearance

- **Language**: Portuguese, English, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
  The switch is immediate.
- **Theme**: light, dark or follow the system.
- **Accent color**: a curated set of six accents.
- **Density**: comfortable or compact rows and fields.
- **Details panel**: beside the list or below it.
- **List columns**: show or hide the User, Category and Strength columns.
- **Profile**: presets ("Classic dark", "Light compact", "High contrast")
  and profiles you save yourself that apply the whole look in one click.

## Behavior

- **Automatic lock**: how long of inactivity before the vault locks.
- **Clipboard clearing**: how many seconds after copying a password it is
  wiped from the clipboard.
- **Track usage history**: records the date of the last copy of each
  credential, which feeds the [Security report](seguranca).
- **Check for updates**: see [Privacy and network](privacidade-rede).

## Accessibility

- **Color blindness**: alternative palettes for protanopia, deuteranopia,
  tritanopia and monochromacy.
- **Text size**: scales the whole interface.
- **High contrast**: reinforces borders and text.
- **Reduce motion**: turns off transitions.
- **Screen reader**: improves announcements for assistive technologies.
